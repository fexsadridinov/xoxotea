# XoXo Tea review package

The build leads with a single drink and a calm tea-green/paper identity, then moves into a sourced market case, editable unit economics, store formats and a meeting request. Ukrainian is the default; English preserves every route. Architectural and product imagery illustrate a pre-launch concept. The financial model exposes assumptions rather than implying operating traction.

The implementation sequence was content/model → responsive routes → generated media/PDFs → browser, keyboard, form and performance verification → review branch. Remaining gates are explicit: strict mobile LCP, real lead delivery, owner evidence and final asset continuity review.
