# Source-led product revision

The local `Sources/` library holds four originals, checksum manifest, extraction, a full manual index and Russian working technical cards. The entire library is ignored by Git and Vercel. No original source, OCR or internal working recipe is deployed. Public menu provenance references physical pages of the owner-provided recipe manual; authorship has not been independently authenticated.

`content/inventory.json` is the canonical menu for all three languages and downloadable one-pagers: six core recipes and four seasonal recipes. Core prioritizes reusable green tea, Four Seasons, Ruby dairy tea, matcha, foam and pearl preparations. Seasonal fruit recipes share those bases. Recipe details retain Chinese labels, source pages, ingredients and explicit adaptations. Prices and calories are intentionally absent until costing and recipe validation.

Adaptations: C03 removes brulee and uses fresh dairy base; C04 removes brulee/torching and selects a 500 ml pilot cup; S02 consolidates regional peach cultivars; S03 replaces osmanthus oolong with Four Seasons. Brown sugar milk has no tea; matcha has no pearls; mango has no coconut. Fruit formats are 700 ml, rather than a uniform 500 ml. Syrup omission is not a sugar-free claim.

The store proposal informs pale stone, layered translucent surfaces, greenery and social space. Its pictures, brand logos and architecture are not reproduced. The file called HeyTea Brandbook contains the same 39-page store proposal text, not a separate identity system. XoXo remains independent.

All ten cards are pilot recipes. Unspecified spoon/foam portions, extraction gaps, ingredient substitutions, local handling requirements and per-recipe costing must be resolved before operational release. Seasonal means supply-qualified rotation, not current stock. The investor model remains a scenario model; its 56 UAH ingredient/packaging cost is not validated against this new menu.

The former generated gallery and CupSequence assets are preserved in the repository but are not used to illustrate the revised catalogue. New schematic glass artwork is ingredient-aware and makes no claim to be finished product photography.
